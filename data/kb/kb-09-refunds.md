# Refunds

**Applies to:** All plans · **Last updated:** November 2026

## Duplicate charges

If your card was charged twice for the same subscription (same amount, two
different dates, one subscription), contact support with the invoice number.
We will:

1. Verify both transactions in the billing record (dates + payment intent IDs
   must differ).
2. Refund one charge through the payment processor.
3. Send you written confirmation of the refund.

Refunds take **5–10 business days** to appear on your statement, depending on
your bank. The refunded amount is the duplicate charge in full.

## Proration credits are not refunds

If an invoice is wrong because a mid-cycle plan change missed its proration
credit, that is corrected by applying a credit and reissuing the invoice — see
"Proration and Invoice Corrections After a Mid-Cycle Plan Change". It is not
a duplicate charge.

## Oversold orders (inventory)

If a sync failure caused overselling, refunds for cancelled orders are issued
from **Orders > select the order > Refund**. The customer is notified
automatically. Fix the underlying sync problem first (see "Shopify Inventory
Sync and Rate Limits") so new oversells stop.

## Checking whether anyone else is affected

For duplicate charges, support checks the same billing batch (same dates) for
other affected customers and proactively credits them. You do not need to
check this yourself.
