# GDPR Erasure Requests (Right to be Forgotten)

**Applies to:** All plans (EU customers) · **Last updated:** November 2026

## What Article 17 requires

Under GDPR Article 17, a data subject can request the complete erasure of
their personal data. OrderLoop processes these requests for merchants acting
on behalf of their customers.

## How to submit a request

Email support (or your CSM) with:

- The data subject's identifying information (name + email).
- Your reference number, if you have one.
- Confirmation that you are authorised to act for the data subject (e.g. you
  are their Data Protection Officer).

## What happens next

1. Support verifies the requester's identity and authority.
2. A **compliance ticket** is created (not a regular support ticket) and you
   receive a reference number.
3. Personal data is erased from the dashboard, support tickets, and scheduled
   backups. **Exceptions:** data that must be kept by law — for example tax
   invoices, which are retained for 7–10 years depending on jurisdiction —
   is **anonymised**, not deleted.
4. You receive written confirmation stating the scope of deletion, the
   retention exceptions applied, and the completion date.
5. The whole process is recorded in the audit log.

## Timeline

Erasure is completed within **30 days** of a verified request, as required by
GDPR. If the request is complex, we will tell you within the first 30 days
and complete it within 60 days.
