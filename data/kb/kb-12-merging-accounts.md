# Merging Two OrderLoop Accounts

**Applies to:** All plans · **Last updated:** November 2026

## When you need a merge

Branch expansion sometimes leaves a business with two OrderLoop accounts (for
example, one per store). They can be merged into a single account so both
stores, products, and order history live under one login.

## What can and cannot be merged

**Can be merged:**

- Stores, products, inventory, and order history from both accounts.
- Staff accounts (they are re-invited under the surviving account).

**Cannot be merged:**

- Billing and invoice history stays with the original account (tax records
  must not be rewritten).
- Each account's API keys and webhook endpoints must be re-created under the
  surviving account.

## Steps

1. Confirm you own both accounts — we send a verification email to the owner
   address of **each** account.
2. Decide which account survives (usually the oldest, or the one on the
   higher plan).
3. Schedule the migration outside peak hours; the merge itself takes under an
   hour, but plan for a verification window.
4. Support runs the merge with the admin tool and verifies that both stores
   appear in the surviving account's dashboard with order history intact.
5. The second account is deactivated after verification.

## Before you ask for a merge

- Export anything you want to keep from the account that will be deactivated
  (reports, API keys).
- Tell your team the date, so nobody is surprised by a login change.
